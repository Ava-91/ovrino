# Product Direction

## What is Ovrino?

Ovrino is a text-to-speech app focused on giving users meaningful control over how text sounds.

The core idea is simple:

> Give your words a voice.

## Use cases

- Listening to written material
- Language learning
- English listening exercises
- Studying
- Accessibility and reading assistance
- Narration
- Creative projects
- Background voiceovers for short-form video
- Casual listening

## Differentiation

Ovrino should not compete only on having "AI voices." Its product identity comes from making voice choice understandable, discoverable, and useful.

The experience should make it easy to answer:

1. What voice do I want?
2. What does it sound like?
3. What settings should I use?
4. How quickly can I hear my text?

## Product principles

- Simple first interaction
- Rich options without overwhelming new users
- Voice previews before generation
- Transparent limits and errors
- Useful defaults
- Accessibility as a first-class concern
- No unnecessary account requirement for the basic experience
